Editorial standards

How we test at CrochetPatternFinder

Every product, pattern, or pick on this site goes through the same review process. We disclose what we paid, how long we tested, and what bias we tried to remove. If a step in this list does not match what you are reading, that is a bug — tell us and we will fix it or pull the post.

How we test

  • Step 1

    We crochet every pattern we review from start to finish — no review based on screenshots or designer copy.

  • Step 2

    Each review notes skill level honestly (beginner / advanced beginner / intermediate / advanced) using craft-yarn-council standards, not the designer's claim.

  • Step 3

    Yarn substitutions are tested on at least one alternate skein and reviewed for gauge match, hand, and color stability.

  • Step 4

    Hook recommendations are based on the actual finished fabric — drape, stitch definition, hand fatigue — not on the designer's first choice.

  • Step 5

    Designer relationships are disclosed; if we received a free pattern from a designer we mark it on the post and don't change the verdict because of it.

  • Step 6

    Reader fixes for tricky rows are credited and folded back into the post so the next crocheter doesn't hit the same wall.
